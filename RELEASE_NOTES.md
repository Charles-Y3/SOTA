Version 2.3.0 key features:

- New Live Translate tab: speak, and read the translation sentence by sentence. Speech is recognized by SenseVoice and translated on your own computer by Hy-MT2-1.8B (nothing is sent anywhere).
- Subtitles for an audience: text-only, rolling captions at the bottom of a projector or second screen, with live original words and streaming translation. Choose 3 lines, 4 lines, or a translation scroll with a live strip.
- Word List for names and jargon, with a downloadable Excel template.

Updates from previous version:

- Live Translate has two priorities: Low latency (default; translates each clause while you are still speaking) and Accuracy (waits for a short pause and uses the previous sentence as context).
- Subtitles open automatically when you press Start (Hide subtitles turns them off). Settings: layout, scroll lines (2, 3 or 4), show original or translation only, overall size, original-line size, which screen.
- Word List: fixes misheard names automatically, forces your own translation for a term, and imports/exports Excel (.xlsx) or text files.
- Quiet voices work: speech detection adapts to the room and boosts quiet microphones, so soft speakers are no longer treated as silence.
- A failed or empty translation is retried once and then marked, instead of leaving a sentence blank.
- Live Translate cannot run together with Live Transcription (both use the microphone and the same speech engine).
